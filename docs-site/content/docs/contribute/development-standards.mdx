# Development Standards

Juno changes should be testable, privacy-aware, and clear to users.

## Standards

1. Preserve the canonical dictation path: shortcut, permission/capability checks, local capture, local engine, safe insertion or copy-ready output.
2. Keep user-facing language free of internal terms such as broker, workbench, venv, repo root, and Python.
3. Do not show `Listening` before real microphone frames and speech energy exist.
4. Do not paste into secure/password fields.
5. Do not save context, learning, history, audio, or paste output when secure-field policy suppresses it.
6. Keep selected-text editing final-only.
7. Require local token auth for mutating routes unless explicitly in development bypass mode.
8. Keep storage under canonical Juno support paths unless a test override is intentional.
9. Add tests for engine behavior and manual QA notes for Mac GUI behavior.
10. Keep docs mechanism-first and aligned with actual code.

## Review bar

A change should explain what changed, why it changed, how it was tested, what can fail, and what user-facing behavior is affected.
