# Transcription Accuracy

Start by identifying the failure type.

## Category 1: Unexpected words

Juno produces text that was not present in your speech — random words, fragments, or filler appearing during silence.

**Cause:** The local speech engine fills silence or background noise with plausible word sequences. This is a known behavior of on-device speech models when audio contains low-level noise without clear speech.

**Fixes:**
- Shorten pause sensitivity in Settings so Juno finalizes and stops sooner instead of continuing to process ambient audio.
- Move to a quieter environment or speak closer to the microphone.
- Move closer to the microphone — Juno is amplitude-sensitive, and quiet speech in a noisy room is the most common cause of plausible-sounding hallucinations from filler audio.
- Check that the correct input device is selected and that no other app is capturing the microphone.

## Category 2: Proper nouns, names, and technical terms are wrong

Common names, product names, acronyms, and domain-specific terms are consistently misheard or substituted.

**Cause:** Generic speech models are not trained on your vocabulary. They default to common alternatives when a term is unfamiliar.

**Fix:** Add the term to your Vocabulary in **Settings → Dictionary and Memory**. Start with the highest-impact terms: people you mention often, your product or company name, repo or project names, and repeated technical acronyms. Most accuracy problems in domain-specific work are solved by a focused vocabulary list of 20–50 terms.

## Category 3: Correct words but wrong punctuation or capitalization

Sentences run together, capitalization is off, or punctuation is missing where you expect it.

**Cause:** The local engine infers punctuation from speech rhythm and pauses. Fast speech or quiet endings reduce confidence.

**Fix:** Speak with slightly more deliberate phrasing, pause briefly between sentences, and end sentences with a slightly lower tone. You can also say punctuation explicitly ("period," "comma," "new paragraph") if consistent freeform punctuation matters.

## Category 4: Output shape is wrong (wrong style)

You get formal prose when you wanted casual text, or bullets when you wanted a paragraph.

**Cause:** A transformation style is active.

**Fix:** Check the active style in the Juno popover. Switch to **Default** or **Casual chat** for plain cleanup. Transformation styles (Formal email, Structured notes, Polished rewrite) reshape output intentionally. If one is set, it applies to every dictation in that app.

## Category 5: Specific app always produces bad results

Transcription is fine in Notes or Mail but breaks in a specific app.

**Cause:** The app provides context that misleads the writing model, or its paste behavior transforms text after insertion.

**Fix:** Run the clean test in Notes first to confirm Juno and the engine are working. If Notes works, use **Per-app writing** to disable Smart Context for the problem app and test again. If the output still looks wrong, switch that app to a plain style to rule out a context or transform issue.

## When to collect a support bundle

If none of the above applies and the problem persists across a clean test in Notes, collect a support bundle with:
- The exact HUD state at the time of the bad output
- App version and macOS version
- A brief description of what you said vs. what appeared

See [Support Bundle](/docs/troubleshooting/support-bundle) for what to include.
