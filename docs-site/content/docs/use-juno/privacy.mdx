# Privacy

Privacy shows what is stored locally and gives one-click controls to clear it.

## What is on the page

**Headline — "Everything stays on your Mac."**
No cloud, no account. Audio, transcripts, vocabulary, corrections, and settings all live on this Mac.

**Audio preview row.**
A waveform of the most recent session with its transcript displayed alongside. The audio shown was never uploaded.

**On this Mac, right now.**
A two-row counter showing exactly what is locally retained at this moment:

- **Dictation history** — the count of transcripts currently in History. Click **Clear** to wipe them all immediately.
- **Audio recordings** — the count of retained audio files. Click **Delete** to remove all audio (transcripts stay; only the audio is removed).

These actions are immediate and not reversible. They obey retention settings.

**Read the full privacy policy.**
Link to the full written policy. Use this when you need to point a teammate or a compliance reviewer at the exact wording.

## What is enforced regardless of any setting

These are product rules, not optional toggles:

- **Secure fields block everything.** Any field macOS reports as a password input forces context, learning, history, recording save, and paste off for that session. The HUD shows **Blocked**, not Listening.
- **No training on your data.** Your audio, transcripts, corrections, and Dictionary & Memory entries are never used to train models. There is no opt-in.
- **No account, no cloud signup.** Juno does not have a server-side account system. You cannot accidentally share data by being signed in.
- **Audio for transcription is held in memory, not on disk, until you confirm retention.** If Recordings is set to Never, audio is discarded the moment transcription finishes.

## When to come here

- After dictating something sensitive — confirm it is not in History, or click Clear.
- Before handing your Mac to someone else — check the counts, clear if needed.
- When introducing Juno to a teammate or compliance reviewer — Privacy is the one-screen summary of local data handling.
- For compliance review, use the retention counters and policy link to explain where audio and transcripts are stored.

For the deeper policy and the per-app override controls, see [Privacy and Data Overview](/docs/privacy-and-data/overview) and [Per-app writing](/docs/use-juno/per-app-writing).
